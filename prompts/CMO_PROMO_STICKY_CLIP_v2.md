## 0. What "sticky" means here
Three jobs, in this order:
1. **Retention** — a new proof every 3–4 s, each one a real recorded number, so there is never a beat to swipe on. Target: 3-second hold ≥ 70 %, average view ≥ 55 % (PostHog/YouTube analytics, first 7 days).
2. **Pinned** — this is the clip pinned to the top of @biddeedai and embedded on `/` hero, `/pricing`, and the sidebar of every public `/deal/*` page below the 32-second property reel.
3. **Magnet** — every proof beat is a screenshot-faithful rendering of a page the viewer can open today; the deal page is the lead magnet (M9), so two live deal pages are shown by address.

## 3. Sticky mechanics (what the Director checks beyond v1 §5)
- Pattern interrupt every ≤ 4 s: cut or a counter landing; no static hold > 3 s except the end card.
- Every number animates in (count-up ≤ 0.6 s) and lands on the exact site figure; OCR of the landed frame must equal the table above.
- Loop point: end card fades to the 0.0 s hook colour field so an autoplay loop reads as continuous (feeds re-count the loop as watch time).
- Captions: whisperX word-level, `#0A2540` on white pill, max 2 lines, safe-area 96 px top / 260 px bottom (platform UI).
- Two end-card variants rendered from one composition: (a) `/r/<code>` → `/` for the pinned/site copy; (b) `/r/<code>` → `/deal/broward/CACE-24-008115` for the deal-page embed (M9: property context lands on the property).
- Also emit a 30 s trim (drop beats 29–36.5) for feeds that punish > 35 s; same code, `?v=30` UTM.
